Founder & owner · Sherora Flowers

Sher Singh Rajawat is the founder and owner of Sherora Flowers, a new floral venture taking shape in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh.

His vision for Sherora is a welcoming space for thoughtful gestures, meaningful celebrations, and the simple joy of flowers. The journey begins with the website, followed by a physical shop in Gwalior—two beginnings, brought together by one personal vision.

As Sherora prepares to open, Sher looks forward to sharing this new chapter with the community.
